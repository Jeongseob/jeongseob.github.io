Jeongseob Ahn (안정섭)

I am an Associate Professor of the Department of Software & Computer Engineering and also affiliated with the Department of Artificial Intelligence at Ajou University. I am interested in building high performance cloud (& datacenter) systems with cross-layer optimization. Before joining Ajou, I contributed in building a large-scale data analytics system with a specialized hardware (called RAPID) at Oracle Labs, California.

Research Group: Computer Systems Laboratory (연구실 학생 모집 안내, 참고자료)
