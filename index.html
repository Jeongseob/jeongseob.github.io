Short Bio

I am an associate professor in the School of Electrical Engineering (Computer Division) and am also affiliated with the Department of Communications Engineering at Korea University. I am interested in building high-performance computer systems. Before joining Korea University, I spent six and a half years at Ajou University. Prior to joining Ajou, I contributed to building a large-scale data analytics system with specialized hardware (called RAPID) at Oracle Labs. I finished my PhD in computer science at KAIST in 2015.
