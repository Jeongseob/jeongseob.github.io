Jeongseob Ahn (안정섭)

I am an Assistant Professor of the Department of Software & Computer Engineering and also affiliated with the Department of Artificial Intelligence at Ajou University. I am interested in building high performance cloud (& datacenter) architecture with cross-layer system design. Before joining Ajou, I participated in building a large-scale data analytics system with a specialized hardware (called RAPID) at Oracle Labs.

Research Group: Computer Systems Laboratory (연구실 학생 모집 안내)
